Our Foundation

Six years of turning
curiosity into capability.

The MPSC IT Club was officially founded in 2019, driven by a collective vision to empower students with the skills and mindset needed to thrive in an increasingly digital world. What began as a small initiative quickly became a dynamic platform where tech enthusiasts could explore programming, robotics, graphic design, and everything IT — beyond the classroom.

From its earliest days, the club has bridged the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world application. Under the guidance of passionate moderators and dedicated executive committees, the club has organised workshops, competitions, and collaborative projects that sparked genuine innovation among its members.

Over the years, MPSC IT Club has grown not just in size but in scope — adapting to emerging technologies and redefining what a student-led tech initiative can achieve. Its legacy rests on teamwork, creativity, and a shared love for technology.

01 / landmark

1st IT Carnival

The beginning of the MPSC IT Club journey. The founding members introduced the first-ever IT Carnival, featuring student projects, technology exhibitions, and innovative ideas that inspired a culture of creativity.

02 / momentum

2nd IT Carnival

Building on the success of the first edition, the second IT Carnival expanded participation and strengthened the club's identity as a home for young innovators and future technologists.

03 / revival

03 / 3rd IT Carnival

After the pandemic pause, the club returned stronger than ever. The 3rd IT Carnival revived the spirit of innovation through exhibitions, competitions, and renewed student engagement.

04 / breakthrough

TECH ODYSSEY

A milestone that united students across multiple wings. Tech Odyssey encouraged collaboration, healthy competition, and groundbreaking projects while setting a new benchmark for future events.

The Journey Continues

FROM A SIMPLE IDEA is now a TO A LIVING LEGACY.

The journey of the MPSC IT Club from its inception in 2019 to its present form has been one of growth, passion, and relentless pursuit of innovation. Through workshops, seminars, events, and national-level participation, the club has continuously empowered students to explore the evolving world of technology beyond textbooks.

Each executive committee, moderator, and mentor has played a pivotal role in shaping this legacy, while every event and initiative has added a new line of code to the club's identity. What began as a simple idea has now become a structured platform for learning, creativity, and leadership.

As the club continues to evolve, its mission remains the same — to inspire, to build, and to lead the next generation of thinkers, coders, and creators.
